05 / FAQ

Know the
protocol.

What is Proof of Useful Compute?

A mining mechanism that rewards real AI inference and training work instead of arbitrary hashes. Benthic meters the work, verifies the result and pays verified contributions.

Who can become a miner?

The reference network is designed for operators with compatible GPUs or accelerators. Testnet requirements will be published as the client matures.

How are fake results stopped?

Jobs are sampled for redundant execution and pass through a bonded challenge window. Slashing is calibrated above the expected gain from cheating.

What is the Trench?

The Trench is the open mining pool that accounts for verified work shares and splits each epoch reward transparently.

Where do rewards settle?

Compact signed epoch results are designed to settle on Robinhood Chain, an EVM-compatible Layer 2.
